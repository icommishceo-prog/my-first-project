About Us

Helping Patients Recover with Comfort and Confidence

Locally based in Bessemer, Alabama, Jett Medical Company is committed to making post-injury recovery easier — one supply, one device, one home setup at a time.

Who We Are

Jett Medical Company is a medical supply and equipment provider specializing in post-injury recovery support. We serve patients, families, and caregivers throughout Bessemer and the surrounding communities with a focus on quality products, dependable service, and personal attention.

From everyday medical and surgical supplies to mobility equipment that restores independence, our goal is to remove obstacles that stand between you and your recovery.

What Drives Us

Recovery looks different for every patient. That's why we emphasize service alongside supply — taking time to help you understand your equipment, configuring it properly in your home, and being available when something needs adjusting or repairing.

We're listed with the Better Business Bureau, CareCredit, and MapQuest, reflecting an established local presence and easy access to financing through CareCredit when you need it.
